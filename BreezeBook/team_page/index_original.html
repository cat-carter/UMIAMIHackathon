Venuvo is a curated, hotel-partner marketplace. Hotels choose to list their spaces; planners see transparent, hotel-supplied details. We're starting with corporate and organizational buyers, then expanding to private celebrations.

01Showcase approved room dimensions, capacities, layouts and services.

03Collect complete briefs before your sales team steps in.
04Track inquiries, conversion and future booking revenue.

A partner-funded marketplace

Our proposed model: start hotels with 2–3 introductory events, then a listing subscription plus a transparent fee on completed bookings. Optional sponsored placement must be clearly labeled and never secretly override suitability ranking.

Business model is a proposal, not an active billing program. Hotel partners must provide/approve accurate details and policies.
