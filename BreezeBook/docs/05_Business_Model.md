# 5. Who Pays and Why It's Worth It

## Why hotels care

- Empty event space still costs money: power, AC, cleaning and upkeep. U.S. hotel utilities run about **$2,478 per room each year** (CBRE, 2025).
- Filling empty time is valuable. One hotel executive estimates that **+10 points of meeting-room use at a 10-room hotel ≈ $2 million a year**.
- Event business is profitable: the hotel food-and-beverage department keeps about **29 cents of profit per dollar** (CBRE, 2025).

## How BreezeBook makes money (hotels pay, planners use it free)
| What | How it works | Price (our assumption) |
|---|---|---|
| Free start | A hotel's first 2–3 events through BreezeBook are free | $0 |
| Subscription | Listing, matching, dashboard, structured requests | $299 per hotel per month |
| Booking fee | Only when an event is booked through us | 3% of the booking |
| Measure + 3D scan | We measure each space on site | $375 per space, one time |
| Featured spot | Show up first, for example in slow summer weeks | $150 per month |
| Negotiation assistant | AI that bargains for the hotel using its rules | Premium add-on (priced in the pilot) |

## The math for one hotel (example)
4 spaces, space use goes from 40% to 45%, $1,500 per booking:

- **Extra revenue:** $219,000 a year. **Extra profit:** about $64,000. **Staff time saved:** about $29,000.
- **Pays BreezeBook:** about $11,700 a year.
- **Net gain:** about $81,000 a year, which is **6.9×** what it pays. If the gains are only half that: **4.5×**.

## The math for us (year 1, 20 Miami hotels)
**$242,000** in revenue, or about $121,000 if only 10 hotels sign up.
