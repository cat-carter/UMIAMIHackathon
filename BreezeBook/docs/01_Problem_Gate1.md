# 1. The Problem (Gate 1)

## Who has the problem?

- **Planners:** people organizing weddings, birthdays, galas, team offsites and conferences in Miami.
- **Hotels:** the events and sales teams who rent out ballrooms, meeting rooms, rooftops, gardens and terraces.

## What's going wrong?
1. **Hotel event spaces sit empty.** Meeting rooms are booked only about 40% of the time, so they're empty about 60% of the time. That figure is an industry estimate from a hotel executive. Miami also has a slow summer: hotel occupancy is forecast at 81.9% in the first quarter but 67.7% in the third.
2. **Planners can't trust what hotels post online.** In one national study, only 11 of 128 hotel websites had meeting information the author could trust. One hotel listed four different sizes for the same space. Planners say room specs (50%), photos (48%) and floor plans (46%) decide which hotels they even contact.
3. **Booking is slow.** Hotels take 5.08 days on average to answer a request. 39% of planners for small meetings give up on a venue that hasn't replied within 2 days.
4. **Planners waste time and travel.** Most planners spend up to 5 hours sourcing each event, and 56% of events need an in-person site visit.

## How things work today (the baseline)
A planner searches several websites, emails a few hotels, waits days for replies, visits in person, and still isn't sure of the real size or final price. Meanwhile, the hotel's space sits empty.

## Our idea (the value hypothesis)
> For Miami event planners and hotels, BreezeBook will turn a plain-language description into a shortlist of measured, fairly priced spaces in seconds, and help hotels fill empty dates. We aim to raise space use by 5 percentage points and cut first replies from about 5 days to under 1 hour.

## What we are building this weekend (one story)
A planner describes an event → BreezeBook reads it → shows the best 3 measured spaces with a 3D view and a price for the date → the planner makes an offer → the hotel's AI assistant counters inside the hotel's rules → the deal goes to the hotel for approval.

## Not this weekend
Live hotel booking systems, payments, real 360° camera scans and a Pinterest sign-in. These are on the roadmap.
